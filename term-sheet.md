# Co-Investment Term Sheet — Rental Property Partnership

**Status:** Draft for discussion. Not legally binding until executed in a definitive agreement reviewed by an Idaho-licensed real-estate attorney.

---

## 1. Parties

- **Owner:** [Brother's full legal name] ("Owner") — holds title to the Property.
- **Investor:** [Your full legal name] ("Investor") — contributes capital.

## 2. Property

- **Address:** [TBD], Idaho
- **Current value (agreed):** $720,000
- **Outstanding mortgage balance:** $278,000
- **Current equity (agreed):** $442,000
- **Mortgage:** 3% interest, ~$1,296/mo P&I, held in Owner's name personally; remains Owner's sole obligation.
- **Insurance:** $1,500/yr (Owner pays).
- **Property tax:** $2,000/yr (Owner pays).

## 3. Capital Contribution

- Investor contributes **$40,000** at execution.
- Wired or delivered as a single payment to Owner or to a designated escrow account.

## 4. Investor's Interest

- **Equity Percentage: 9.05%** of the Property's equity, fixed at execution. ($40,000 / $442,000 = 9.05%.)
- The Equity Percentage is **not subject to dilution** by future contributions, refinancings, or expenses paid by Owner.
- The interest is **economic, not titled.** Investor does not appear on the deed. Title remains with Owner.

## 5. Security for Principal

- Owner shall execute a **Promissory Note** in the amount of $40,000 in favor of Investor.
- The Note shall be secured by a **Deed of Trust on the Property recorded in [County] County, Idaho**, junior to the existing first mortgage.
- The Deed of Trust secures the $40,000 principal only. The equity upside in Section 7 is contractual.

## 6. Preferred Cash Return

- Investor receives a **5% per annum preferred cash return** on the $40,000 contribution = **$2,000/year**.
- Paid **quarterly in arrears** ($500 per quarter) on the 15th day of the month following each calendar quarter end.
- **Cumulative:** any quarterly payment not made on time accrues and must be paid in full (without interest on arrears) before any distribution to Owner from refinance or sale proceeds.
- Funded from rental income; if rental income is insufficient in a given quarter, the unpaid amount accrues per the cumulative provision above.

## 7. Distribution Events

### 7.1 Cash-Out Refinance
If Owner executes a cash-out refinance, Investor receives **9.05% of the net cash-out proceeds** (gross cash-out minus refinance closing costs) within 10 business days of funding. Investor's $40,000 principal and Equity Percentage remain unchanged after the refinance.

### 7.2 Sale of Property
On sale, the proceeds shall be distributed in the following order:
1. Pay off the first mortgage and any senior liens.
2. Pay closing costs, agent commissions, and seller-side transfer expenses.
3. Pay Investor any accrued and unpaid preferred return.
4. Repay Investor's $40,000 principal (in full satisfaction of the Promissory Note).
5. Remaining net proceeds: **9.05% to Investor, 90.95% to Owner.**

### 7.3 Mandatory Liquidity (10-Year Trigger)
At any time after the 10-year anniversary of execution, **Investor may, by written notice, require Owner to either:**
- Execute a cash-out refinance sufficient to return Investor's $40,000 + accrued return + 9.05% of then-current equity above the original $442,000 baseline; **or**
- List the Property for sale at fair market value within 90 days.

Owner chooses which path. If Owner cannot or does not act within 180 days of notice, Investor may compel a sale through the dispute-resolution process in Section 12.

## 8. Operations and Control

- Owner has **sole operational control**: tenant selection, rent setting, repairs, capital improvements, refinancing decisions, and day-to-day management.
- Owner shall **provide Investor with annual statements** showing rental income, expenses, mortgage balance, and an estimate of Property value (Zillow/Redfin acceptable; appraisal at distribution events).
- **Material decisions requiring Investor's written consent:**
  - Sale of the Property before Year 10.
  - Refinance that increases the loan balance above the current $278,000 by more than 10%.
  - Encumbering the Property with additional liens senior to Investor's Deed of Trust.
  - Leasing the Property to a relative below market rent.

## 9. Transfer / Death / Divorce

- **Death of Investor:** Investor's interest passes to Investor's spouse, [Wife's full legal name], with all rights and obligations intact. If spouse predeceases or declines, the interest passes per Investor's will/trust.
- **Death of Owner:** Owner's heirs assume Owner's obligations. Investor retains all rights and may, at Investor's option, accelerate the 10-year mandatory liquidity right.
- **Divorce of Owner:** Owner's spousal interests, if any, are subordinate to this Agreement. Owner shall obtain spousal consent at execution if married.
- **Right of First Refusal:** Neither party may transfer their interest to a third party without first offering it to the other party on the same terms, with 30 days to accept.

## 10. Default

- Failure by Owner to (a) make the preferred return for 4 consecutive quarters, (b) maintain the first mortgage in good standing, (c) maintain insurance, or (d) pay property taxes constitutes a default.
- On default, Investor may (i) accelerate the Note and foreclose on the Deed of Trust, (ii) compel a sale, or (iii) both.

## 11. Tax Treatment

- This arrangement is intended to be treated as a **debt-plus-equity-kicker** for tax purposes, not a partnership.
- Investor reports the 5% preferred return as interest income.
- Investor reports the 9.05% equity distributions as capital gain at the distribution event.
- Owner retains all depreciation deductions and reports rental income/expense on Schedule E.
- Both parties acknowledge they have been advised to consult their own tax advisors. This section is not tax advice.

## 12. Dispute Resolution

- Parties shall first attempt good-faith negotiation, then non-binding mediation in [County], Idaho.
- Unresolved disputes shall be settled by binding arbitration under the rules of the American Arbitration Association, seated in Idaho.
- Prevailing party recovers reasonable attorney's fees.

State of Idaho.
